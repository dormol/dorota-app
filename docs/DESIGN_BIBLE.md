# CHAPTER 1

## THE CAMPUS

This document defines the visual identity, architecture, atmosphere, and design language of Art Mentor Studio.

Every future illustration, screen, building, interior, and user experience should follow the principles described here.

# The Goal and The Meaning of the Campus

Art Mentor Studio Campus is the place where an artist's deepest creative aspirations find their home, where artistic dreams become reality through dedication, passion, and creation.

The Campus is conceived as a unique artistic sanctuary dedicated to the development of every artist's individual voice, creative identity, and lifelong artistic journey.

Its purpose is to provide a place where artists can fully immerse themselves in the process of creation, surrounded by inspiration, knowledge, beauty, and an environment designed to support artistic growth.

The Campus brings together artistic tradition, contemporary knowledge, and human imagination. It respects the heritage of great artistic institutions while creating a new chapter in the history of artistic development.
For every artist who arrives at the Campus, this place represents more than a studio or a school. It is a destination created for discovering one's artistic potential, developing personal expression, and transforming ideas into meaningful works of art.

The Campus exists as a place where creativity becomes a way of life — where artists learn, practice, experiment, and create while remaining connected to their own unique vision.

The ultimate goal of Art Mentor Studio Campus is to help every artist discover, develop, and share their artistic voice.

# The Location of The Campus

Art Mentor Studio Campus is located on Mars, within a unique settlement known as Dreamland.

The idea of placing the Campus beyond Earth has its origins in a visionary concept developed approximately thirty-five years ago by Mgr. Eng. Jan Mol, an environmental protection specialist, who imagined future travel to Mars while living in Florida.

His concept focused on the idea of a future travel agency dedicated to organizing journeys to Mars and registering people interested in becoming part of this extraordinary form of human exploration.

At that time, organized travel to Mars belonged to the realm of imagination and long-term human aspirations. This vision reflected curiosity about the future of exploration and the human desire to discover new horizons.

This early vision of my husband became the inspiration for me to create a place where human exploration and artistic development exist in harmony.

Art Mentor Studio Campus represents the connection between exploration and creativity — a place where the spirit of discovery meets the world of art.

The location of the Campus reflects a personal vision of combining exploration, environmental awareness, architecture, and artistic expression. Mars becomes not only the setting of the Campus, but also a symbol of imagination, courage, and the continuous search for new horizons.

The Campus exists within a carefully designed protected environment that provides Earth-like living conditions. This allows artists to live, create, and experience spaces inspired by nature while being part of a new world beyond Earth.

The relationship between the Mars landscape and the artistic spaces of the Campus creates a unique atmosphere — a place where the vastness of space meets human creativity, and where artists can fully dedicate themselves to the development of their artistic voice.
